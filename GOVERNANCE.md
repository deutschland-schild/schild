# Governance

Deutschland-Schild is in alpha and has no legal entity yet.

## Today

The initiator listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) decides on the roadmap, contracts, releases and who gets commit access. Decisions are recorded in the repository. Technical disagreements are discussed in the issue or pull request first.

## Humans decide

Agents may propose and build. Humans decide on kill criteria, security sign-offs and legal questions.

## Sponsors

Money or a paid assignment buys no influence on rules, access or data. Paid work goes through the same review as every other contribution and enters the project under the MIT License.

## Later

The target structure, including the transfer to a neutral host, is described in [docs/concept/05-governance-und-traegerschaft.md](docs/concept/05-governance-und-traegerschaft.md). This file changes when that transfer happens.
